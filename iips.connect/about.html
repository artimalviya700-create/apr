About the institute

A place to learn, lead and make an impact

The International Institute of Professional Studies (IIPS) is a teaching department of Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), Indore. Established in 1992, IIPS brings professional education in management and computer studies together in a university environment.

1992

IIPS is established

IIPS has grown as an academic centre for professional, graduate and postgraduate education. Its cross-disciplinary programmes connect management, technology and practical learning.

1964

DAVV is founded

Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya is a state university based in Indore. IIPS is part of the university’s teaching community and shares its wider academic and research ecosystem.

Learn by connecting

Professional education

Integrated and postgraduate programmes give students opportunities to build subject knowledge, work across disciplines and prepare for changing professional fields.

🎓 VC’s Desk

Hon’ble Vice Chancellor

As Vice-Chancellor, Dr. Rakesh Singhai emphasizes academic distinction and a stronger learning experience for students. His priorities include keeping curricula connected to global trends and industry needs, and supporting interdisciplinary and skill-based education.

DAVV also aims to grow its research environment through focused support, partnerships and innovation, while building wider academic connections. These goals encourage students to learn, explore ideas and prepare for a changing world.
